About

Will your business idea work? Let's find out with numbers.

I'm Omar Sameh, a Business Administration graduate of Alexandria University (2024). I help founders and early-stage teams answer one question before they spend a single pound: will this business work?

What I do

I prepare end-to-end feasibility studies for startups and new projects:

  • Market study: who your target customers are, and where the gaps in the market are.
  • Financial study: startup costs, break-even point and cash requirements, delivered in an editable model.
  • Operational plan: built around the type of business (for example, a service business).
  • Risk assessment: what could go wrong, how likely it is, and how to reduce it.
  • Final recommendation: go, adjust, or stop, with clear reasons.

Problem-solving and market-fit ideas

A good study doesn't just say yes or no. When a project has weak points, I help fix them:

  • Identify the real problem.
  • Study what customers actually want and what competitors fail to offer.
  • Generate alternative concepts.

Experience

I completed a full feasibility study for a specialty coffee shop, covering market research, costing, equipment planning, and financial projections. I now support startups and small businesses that need a clear plan before launching or expanding.

How I work

Every result is reviewable. Assumptions are written down, sources are clear, and the model is yours to edit.

Open to: feasibility studies, business plans, and market-fit reviews for new projects.
